⚕️ Medical Disclaimer
Electra is an educational prototype designed to create general awareness about medicine safety and potential drug interactions. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist before starting, stopping, or changing any medicine — especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, elderly, or living with kidney, liver, or heart disease. Medicines that Electra cannot recognise must be verified with a doctor before use. Never delay or disregard professional medical advice because of anything shown on this screen. The physical compartment lock is a reminder aid, not a medical intervention. In an emergency, call 108 (ambulance) or 112 (national emergency number) immediately.
